Absolutely. Every paid plan includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. You can explore every feature and invite your whole team.

Plans are priced per member, per month. Start with the free Starter plan and upgrade whenever your team is ready.

Can I change plans later?+

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.
